FAQ

  • How many states are there in russia?

    +

    Russia is divided into 85 federal subjects, which are the constituent entities of the Russian Federation.

  • What is a common city to visit in Russia?

    +

    The most commonly visited city in Russia for tourists is St. Petersburg, often referred to as the "Venice of the North" or the "Cultural Capital".

  • What is the Golden Ring in Russia?

    +

    The Golden Ring of Russia is a tourist route that was specifically developed in the late 1960s uniting several mediaeval Russian cities located to the north-east of Moscow.

  • What is the most popular food in russia?

    +

    Pelmeni, small dumplings filled with meat, are widely considered the most popular food in Russia, often referred to as the "heart of Russian cuisine".
